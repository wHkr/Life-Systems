# Startup
---

## ▶️ Startup Procedure

Follow these steps **in order** each time the pool is started after maintenance, seasonal opening, or an extended shutdown.

---

## 📋 Before You Begin

!!! info "What You'll Need"

    - Pool filled to the proper water level
    - All hoses connected securely
    - Electrical power available (120V GFCI)
    - Sand filter assembled and ready
    - QS1200 Saltwater System connected
    - System Diagram available for reference if needed

---

## Step 1 — Inspect the Pool

???+ note "Inspect Before Starting"

    Verify the following before turning anything on:

    - Pool water is at the proper level.
    - No hoses are kinked or pinched.
    - Hose clamps are tight.
    - Return fitting is securely installed.
    - Skimmer/intake is free of debris.
    - Equipment appears clean and undamaged.

---

## Step 2 — Verify Valve Positions

??? note "Check Valves"

    Confirm the following:

    - Suction valves are **OPEN**.
    - Multiport valve is set to **FILTER**.
    - Air relief valve is closed (unless priming the system).

---

## Step 3 — Prime the Pump

??? note "Remove Air From the System"

    If the pump has lost prime:

    1. Fill the pump basket with water.
    2. Open the air relief valve.
    3. Allow trapped air to escape.
    4. Close the valve once water flows steadily.
    5. Ensure the pump basket is completely full before continuing.

---

## Step 4 — Start the Pump

??? note "Begin Water Circulation"

    Turn the pump ON.

    Verify:

    - Water begins circulating.
    - Pump sounds smooth and normal.
    - No excessive air bubbles are visible.
    - No leaks appear around hoses or fittings.

---

## Step 5 — Verify Sand Filter Operation

??? note "Check the Filter"

    Confirm:

    - Strong return flow into the pool.
    - No water leaking around fittings.
    - Filter pressure appears normal.
    - Water is circulating continuously.

---

## Step 6 — Enable the QS1200 Saltwater System

??? note "Start Chlorine Generation"

    After normal water circulation has been established:

    - Turn ON the QS1200.
    - Verify normal indicator lights.
    - Confirm there are no warning or error lights.
    - Verify water is flowing in the correct direction through the unit.

---

## Step 7 — Final Inspection

???+ success "Startup Complete"

    Confirm all of the following:

    - ✅ Pump running smoothly
    - ✅ Strong return flow
    - ✅ No water leaks
    - ✅ No trapped air
    - ✅ QS1200 operating normally
    - ✅ Pool ready for normal operation

---

# Common Startup Problems

??? warning "💧 Pump runs, but no water is moving"

    Check these items in order:

    - Verify pool water level.
    - Ensure both suction valves are open.
    - Prime the pump again.
    - Check hoses for kinks.
    - Inspect intake screens for blockages.

---

??? warning "🔊 Pump is noisy"

    Common causes:

    - Air trapped in the plumbing.
    - Low water level.
    - Loose suction-side fitting.
    - Pump not fully primed.

---

??? warning "🌊 Weak or No Return Flow"

    Possible causes:

    - Dirty sand filter.
    - Pump not primed.
    - Closed valve.
    - Blocked hose.
    - Dirty skimmer basket.

---

??? warning "🧂 QS1200 Will Not Start"

    Verify:

    - Pump is circulating water.
    - Salt level is within specification.
    - Power is connected.
    - Water is flowing in the correct direction.
    - No warning lights are displayed.

---

## ⏱️ Expected Startup Timeline

!!! tip "Normal Startup Timing"

    Approximate startup times:

    **Pump starts:** Immediately

    **Water reaches the return jet:** 30–60 seconds

    **Air clears from the system:** 1–2 minutes

    **QS1200:** Turn ON only after steady water flow has been established.

---

## 💡 Startup Tips

??? tip "Helpful Reminders"

    - Follow the steps in order.
    - Don't rush the priming process.
    - Listen for unusual pump noises.
    - Watch for leaks during the first few minutes.
    - If something doesn't look right, stop and inspect before continuing.
    - Most startup issues are caused by trapped air, low water level, or closed valves.

---

## 🚀 Startup Complete

Once all checks have passed, the system should be operating normally.

If a problem develops after startup:

* 🗺️ Review the **System Diagram**
* ⚠️ Open the **Troubleshooting** guide
* 🧹 Continue to **Maintenance**
* 📦 Check the **Parts Reference**
