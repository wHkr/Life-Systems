# Pool Operator Guide

## What *I* Check vs. What the Equipment Checks

A pool operates best when both the **operator (you)** and the **equipment** do their jobs. The equipment keeps water circulating and producing chlorine, while you ensure the water stays clean, balanced, and safe.

---

# What **I** Check (Operator Responsibilities)

These items require human inspection or routine testing.

## 🌊 Water Level

* Keep the water approximately halfway up the skimmer opening.
* Too low:

  * Pump may suck air.
  * Can lose prime.
  * Risk of pump damage.
* Too high:

  * Skimmer becomes less effective.
  * Surface debris is not removed efficiently.

---

## 🧪 Water Chemistry

Test regularly for:

| Test                | Purpose                                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Free Chlorine       | Sanitizes the pool                                           |
| Combined Chlorine   | Indicates contaminants in the water                          |
| pH                  | Comfort and chlorine effectiveness                           |
| Total Alkalinity    | Stabilizes pH                                                |
| Calcium Hardness    | Protects plaster and equipment                               |
| Cyanuric Acid (CYA) | Protects chlorine from UV sunlight                           |
| Salt Level          | Confirms enough salt is available for the chlorine generator |

---

## 👀 Water Appearance

Visually inspect for:

* Clear water
* Cloudiness
* Algae
* Unusual color
* Ability to clearly see the bottom of the pool

---

## 🍂 Skimmer Basket

Inspect and empty when needed.

Remove:

* Leaves
* Grass
* Bugs
* Small debris

---

## 🧺 Pump Basket

Inspect before the pump.

Remove:

* Leaves
* Pine needles
* Hair
* Large debris

---

## 📈 Filter Pressure

Monitor the pressure gauge.

* Record the pressure after a clean filter.
* When pressure rises approximately **8–10 PSI above the clean reading**, clean or backwash the filter (depending on filter type).

---

## 💧 Water Flow

Check the return jets.

Normal:

* Strong, steady flow.

Weak flow may indicate:

* Dirty filter
* Full baskets
* Closed valve
* Air leak
* Pump problem

---

## 🔍 Equipment Inspection

Look for:

* Water leaks
* Salt deposits
* Rust
* Loose fittings
* Cracked plumbing

---

## 🔊 Pump Sound

Normal:

* Smooth humming

Abnormal:

* Grinding
* Screeching
* Air sucking
* Cavitation
* Loud vibration

---

## ⚡ Salt Cell Condition

Inspect periodically.

Look for:

* Calcium buildup
* White scale
* Dirty plates
* Damaged plates

---

# What the Equipment Checks (Automatic Responsibilities)

---

## 🌀 Pump

The pump does **not** monitor water quality.

Its job is simply to:

* Pull water from the pool.
* Push water through the filter.
* Maintain circulation.

---

## 🔀 Multiport Valve

Checks nothing.

It simply directs water based on the selected position:

* Filter
* Backwash
* Rinse
* Waste
* Recirculate
* Closed

---

## 🧼 Filter

The filter does not electronically monitor anything.

Its job is to:

* Physically trap dirt and debris.
* Allow clean water to continue through the system.

The pressure gauge tells **you** when cleaning is needed.

---

## ⚡ QS1200 Salt Chlorine Generator

The chlorine generator performs several automatic checks.

### ✅ Water Flow

Uses a flow switch.

If there is insufficient water flow:

* Stops chlorine production.
* Protects the cell.

---

### 🧂 Salt Level

Estimates salinity using electrical conductivity.

If salt is outside the operating range, it may display:

* Low Salt
* High Salt

---

### ⚡ Cell Operation

Monitors electrical performance.

Checks:

* Voltage
* Current

If readings are abnormal, it may display:

* Check Cell
* Inspect Cell
* Service Cell

---

### 🔄 Cell Polarity

Many systems periodically reverse electrical polarity.

Purpose:

* Reduce calcium buildup.
* Extend cell life.

---

### ⚙ Chlorine Output

Produces chlorine according to the percentage selected by the operator.

Examples:

* 20%
* 40%
* 60%
* 80%
* 100%

The system cycles the cell on and off to achieve the selected output.

---

### 🌡 Water Temperature (Some Systems)

Some salt systems monitor water temperature.

If the water becomes too cold:

* Chlorine production may stop automatically.
* Protects the salt cell.

# Remember

**Your job:** Ensure the water is clean, balanced, and the equipment is operating properly.

**The equipment's job:** Move water, filter debris, and safely generate chlorine when operating conditions are correct.

The equipment assists you—it does **not** replace routine testing, inspection, and maintenance.
